# Stack pack: flutter — a11y
extends: core/skills/a11y/SKILL.md

## Stack-specific signals
- Tappable `GestureDetector`/`InkWell` wrapping a non-interactive widget with no `Semantics`/`MergeSemantics` → no accessible name/role.
- `Image`/`Icon` without `semanticLabel` (or empty) → unlabeled for screen readers.
- Missing/disabled focus traversal on custom dialogs/menus; `FocusNode` not managed/disposed.
- `ExcludeSemantics`/`BlockSemantics` used to silence the tree without justification.
- Insufficient contrast on `Text`/icons introduced in the diff; tap targets below ~44dp.
- Form fields without an associated label (`TextField` `decoration.labelText`/semantics).

## Stack-specific remedies
- Wrap interactive gestures in `Semantics(button: true, label: ...)`; give every `Image`/`Icon` a `semanticLabel`.
- Manage `FocusNode` (create + dispose); keep semantics; ensure ≥44dp targets and AA contrast.

## Stack-specific severity guidance
- Unlabeled/unfocusable interactive control: High.
- `ExcludeSemantics` hiding core content: High.
- Contrast/tap-target regression: Medium.
