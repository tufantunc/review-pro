# NNNN: Short imperative title

Status: accepted
Date: YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

The forces at play: the problem, the constraints, and why the decision could not wait. Two to five sentences. Link evidence instead of restating it.

## Decision

One or two sentences, active voice: what we chose.

What we rejected, and why, one line per alternative.

## Consequences

What this makes easier. What it makes harder. What it forecloses, and what would have to be true to revisit it.
